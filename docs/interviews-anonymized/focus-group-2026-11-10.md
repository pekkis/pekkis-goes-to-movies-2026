The recording and the full transcript stay outside the repository (`docs/interviews/` is gitignored), and the recording is to be deleted once the notes are done, as promised to the participants.

## Findings

### 1. Film first and time first, both

- **P1 starts from the film:** a film that interests them, then the cinema's site to see whether the best seat in the big hall is free. "Going at random" doesn't happen.
- **P2 starts from the time:** "what starts on Wednesday between five and seven", then the best of what's on. Sometimes a particular film is awaited, and missing it hurts.
- **M is in between:** a list of films, fitted to the calendar.

The service needs both ways in, as built (search by time and place; search by film).

### 2. Films are discovered by chance

Nobody follows anything systematically. Sources named: a YouTube reviewer, algorithmic suggestions ("at the mercy of the algorithm", P1), directors' pages on IMDb, books being adapted, favourite directors and actors, Finnish people on social media who go to the cinema very often, a weekly podcast, Rotten Tomatoes, Letterboxd, and **trailers in the cinema**. Finnkino's "coming soon" page was once a reliable weekly list (M); now its order is random and films disappear and reappear.

### 3. Finnkino's redesign hurts everyone

- Nothing can be found unless you already know the film (P2).
- Buying on the phone failed for P2; they were told to use a computer. Another attempt took 40 minutes and ended with a trip to the cinema to cancel the purchase (M or P2; the transcript doesn't tell).
- People queue at the cinema because they couldn't buy online (P2's observation).
- Smaller chains' and private cinemas' sites are "nicely simple" (BioRex, P2; P1's own cinema).

### 4. The aggregators: in use, made by angry individuals

P2 uses two hobbyist aggregators and prefers the one that lets you choose the city first. P2 believes they are each made by a single person out of frustration with Finnkino ("one of them couldn't buy a ticket, stayed home and made this site"). P1 has only glanced at one made by an acquaintance. Nobody named the operator as a reason to trust or distrust a site.

### 5. Why a wanted screening is missed

- **Hall and seat (P1):** if the best seat in the big hall is taken, the interest drops. The small halls of P1's cinema are less pleasant.
- **Short runs, late or far away (P2):** niche films get one week, late showings (21–22) and a suburban multiplex. P2 wants to be asleep by ten.
- **Not knowing in time (P2, M, P1):** guest screenings and special events are heard of afterwards. A 4K restoration presented by its director abroad, found a month too late ("I could have flown there").
- **Sold out:** P2's knitting screenings are sold out every time, down to the front row edges, before P2 hears of them.
- **Finnkino's club previews (P2):** members get an email about previews with free treats; by the time the email arrives, they are gone. During the session P2 found one with a single seat left.

### 6. The strongest signal: tell me when it's on

Unprompted, P2 asked for a service that reminds them when a knitting screening, a horror festival or a new film by a favourite actor comes on. Later P2 also imagined a service that learns their taste over a year and suggests the next film by a favourite actor. **M then described the planned reminder idea**, so later agreement is partly prompted; the first request was not.

### 7. Programmes come too late

Most cinemas publish the coming week on Tuesday or Wednesday (M, from the data); P1's cinema on Thursday. Four days is too short to organise anything. P2 described trying to take a group of about 15 people to the same screening: one person buys every ticket and collects the money. A way to reserve a block of seats and share links would help, though it is outside what this service does.

### 8. The screening itself matters

Seats (real fabric seats rather than "imitation leather that sucks you in", P2), rustling and snacks during the film, people going to the toilet during licensed screenings, quiet empty halls in the pandemic years, a private hall rental. Ideas: a **quiet screening** ("don't rustle"), screenings **without alcohol**, and as a counterweight **rowdy screenings** of the right films (a sing-along cult classic). Saturday mornings are known to be calm.

### 9. Critics' scores divide, as expected

- **P2:** high scores make them anxious ("now I have to like this"); scores everywhere are meaningless without knowing who gave them.
- **P1:** avoids trailers, which reveal too much and set false expectations; a teaser at most. Has their own starting-at-two-and-a-half-stars system and "mood films" that only work in the right state of mind.
- The best experience of all can come from knowing nothing (a surprise action film; Finnkino's mystery screenings, which P2 tries to guess from the posters).

The question was forgotten from the session and raised by P2 at the end. Opt-in scores are consistent with what they said.

### 10. Chat: rejected for searching, wanted for planning

- **First reaction, negative.** P2: typing on a phone is slow, ticking a few boxes is faster. Another participant (by context P1): it feels like "calling a secretary to find me a film". This matches the questionnaire, where hardly anyone wanted a chat.
- **Then, uses they would want:**
  - a triple-feature day: "combine three films that interest me and fit in a day" (suggested by M, taken up by P1);
  - a festival: the best programme across halls by favourite directors and actors (P1);
  - a mood search: "no war, no testosterone, not many deaths" (P2);
  - a daily message: "what's on after work today" (P2);
  - suggestions of rare cult films to dig through (P1);
  - learning their taste over time, by asking for a rating after each film (P2, who said this changed their mind "in a more positive direction").
- An ordinary moviegoer may not need it; a heavy moviegoer might (by context P1).

The chat doesn't replace the search page; its value is in planning, recommending and telling first.

### 11. The website task: mostly worked

- **The task was completed.** P1 found _Wild Horse Nine_ by typing part of the title, then narrowed with the film facet and the time-of-day facet.
- **The time filter was praised** (P2): Finnkino's site can't search by time of day.
- **"Kaupungit" (Cities) link:** P2 expected it to lead somewhere else; it leads to the front page (the label was wrong).
- **Fuzzy search:** "wild ho" also brought up _Sysimetsä_ (_Wildwood_). P1 understood it after a moment and narrowed it down; it wasn't explained on the page.
- **Wanted filters:** genre ("tonight I want horror", P1), maybe the age rating. "Simple is beautiful" (P1).
- **Not tested:** the phone layout, though P2 uses a phone for nearly everything.

## Against the hypotheses

From the [journey maps](../design/journey-map.md#hypotheses-to-test):

| #   | Hypothesis                                 | Result                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | People check several cinemas' sites        | **Partly.** P1 checks one cinema; P2 uses an aggregator instead of cinemas' sites.                                                  |
| 2   | Language and subtitles matter              | **Unclear:** not raised by anyone.                                                                                                  |
| 3   | Time and place first, or film first        | **Both**, one participant each (finding 1).                                                                                         |
| 4   | Distance and travel time affect the choice | **Weakly supported:** a suburban multiplex late at night is a reason not to go (P2).                                                |
| 5   | Buying at the cinema is acceptable         | **Not asked.** Buying at Finnkino was a strong pain point.                                                                          |
| 6   | People want to be told                     | **Supported, strongly**, unprompted (finding 6).                                                                                    |
| 7   | Critics' scores: wanted or avoided         | **Divides**, as expected (finding 9).                                                                                               |
| 8   | Personal defaults; prints and formats      | **Partly:** the hall and the seat matter to P1. Prints and formats not raised; the probes were not run.                             |
| 9   | People follow kinds of screenings          | **Supported, strongly:** knitting screenings, a horror festival, director visits, club previews, quiet and alcohol-free screenings. |

## New to us

- **Programmes are published too late for planning** (finding 7). We can't fix the cinemas' schedules, but we can show the moment a programme appears, and the far-ahead screenings (premieres, special events) that are already on sale.
- **The hall and the seat** as a reason to go or not.
- **Quiet and rowdy screenings** as kinds of screenings.
- **Finnkino's member previews** that fill before the email arrives.

## What to build first (suggested)

1. **Following kinds of screenings and films** with a notice when one appears (finding 6; hypotheses 6 and 9). The data already has series and tags.
2. **A check of the phone layout** before the next test, and a test on a phone.
3. **Small fixes from the task:** the "Kaupungit" label; say why a fuzzy match was included; consider a genre filter.

## Method: limits to keep in mind

- **The moderator took part in the discussion**, said so ("playing along"), and gave opinions ("Finnkino is hopeless", "our competitors"). Answers may lean towards the moderator's views.
- **The moderator described features before or while participants reacted to them** (the reminder service, the special screenings page), so some agreement is prompted.
- **Both participants know the moderator well**, and the site is the moderator's own. Feedback may be kinder than from strangers.
- **Two participants, both keen**, below the guide's 5–8. P1 said so themselves: an ordinary moviegoer may not need this.
- **One note-taker, who was also the moderator**; notes come from the recording's automatic transcript, which is poor in places. A few quotes are attributed to a participant by context (marked in the quotes file).
- **The critics' scores question was forgotten** and came up only because P2 raised it.
- **Both used a laptop**, against the guide's mix of devices.
