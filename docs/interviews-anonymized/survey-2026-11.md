# Survey, October–November 2026: findings

The questionnaire "How Do You Find Movies in Finland" (Microsoft Forms, English), answered from 2026-10-23 to 2026-11-06. This is the anonymised analysis: no names, email addresses or per-respondent details. The export stays outside the repository (`docs/interviews/`, gitignored). Compare with the [focus group](focus-group-2026-11-10.md).

## The sample

- **23 responses.** One is the maintainer's own; it is left out of every figure here (**n = 22**), because the author answering their own survey biases it. Including it changes little; where it does, it's noted.
- **A convenience sample:** 17 of the 22 answered on a single day (2026-11-03), most likely after one share in a course or work channel.
- **The Helsinki region:** Helsinki 13, Espoo 3; Jyväskylä, Lahti, Oulu and Tampere one each; 2 blank.
- **Mostly occasional moviegoers**, not the hobbyists we design for:

| Cinema visits in the past 12 months | n   | %   |
| ----------------------------------- | --- | --- |
| 1 time                              | 3   | 14% |
| 2–3 times                           | 12  | 55% |
| 4–6 times                           | 2   | 9%  |
| 7 or more times                     | 5   | 23% |

That makes the survey a view of the **general** need, and the focus group a view of the hobbyist's.

**Note: the survey has no bipolar (semantic differential) items.** Its scales are 5-point Likert items (satisfaction, usefulness) and multiple choice. The means below are for those Likert items. A bipolar measure, such as the 8-item short UEQ (UEQ-S) on the prototype, was not run. See [survey-scales.md](survey-scales.md) for what bipolar questions and a table of means are, and how to present ours.

## Open answers: themes

Four open questions:

- what was difficult about finding a screening;
- what is hard to find or compare;
- the one change that would help most;
- what people like or dislike about Finnkino's redesign.

Answers are grouped by theme. The counts are respondents who raised the theme anywhere, so one person can count under several themes. Quotes are verbatim, shortened with "…".

| Theme                                         | Respondents | Quotes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Finnkino's website is hard to use**         | 11          | "slow and complicated. Not enough useful information, and too much unuseful information"; "Learning curve was very steep … Usability is 1/5"; "could and would never use on a phone!"                                                                                                                          |
| **No overview across cinemas**                | 8           | "Having to search different places to get an overview of screening times in all the different theaters"; "Google can aggregate this information if you know which movie you want … if you're only browsing … Google isn't much help"; "you have to be careful to manually pick all the cinemas closest to you" |
| **How screenings are presented and filtered** | 5           | "one big list of films by start time … like in the cinema lobby. Then easy to use filters for location/film/genre … sliders for location radius"; "Entertain movie name you want to see and it gives list of screenings"                                                                                       |
| **Too short a planning horizon**              | 4           | "No times are visible for next week's movies"; "For parents with little KIDS its madness that only one week can be viewed at a time"; "nice to know when it leaves the theaters so I know how much time I have left"                                                                                           |
| **Seats and format**                          | 4           | "Free seats (good seats) at a suitable time. Combining these two criteria with an IMAX screen turned out to be impossible!"; "A better indicator of free seats than '123/234 available'"                                                                                                                       |
| **Buying and tickets**                        | 4           | "it does not show my digital tickets"; reserving and paying at the cinema "used to be a horrible experience"; a payment benefit service; ticket discounts                                                                                                                                                      |

Not everything about Finnkino was negative. Some had got used to it ("I have gotten used to it"), one thought it had improved since launch, and one found the "switch seats" feature on the ticket page useful.

## Against the hypotheses

From the [journey maps](../design/journey-map.md#hypotheses-to-test):

| #   | Hypothesis                                 | Survey result                                                                                                                                             |
| --- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | People check several cinemas' sites        | **Partly.** 45% compared cinemas last time, but 91% use Finnkino and only 23% other cinemas' sites. Lack of an overview is the second biggest open theme. |
| 2   | Language and subtitles matter              | **Not supported** as a top factor: 1 of 22 picked it in their top three. It may be taken for granted rather than unimportant.                             |
| 3   | Time and place first, or film first        | **Contradicted:** **film first, 73%**; time first, 14%. The focus group split one each. Both ways in are needed, but film first is the main one for most. |
| 4   | Distance and travel time affect the choice | **Supported:** location is in 82% of top threes, as often as the start time. Nearly half travel by public transport.                                      |
| 5   | Buying at the cinema is acceptable         | **Not asked.** Buying at Finnkino is one of the open themes.                                                                                              |
| 6   | People want to be told                     | **Not asked.** Related: wanting to see further ahead, and when a film leaves the cinemas.                                                                 |
| 7   | Critics' scores                            | **Not asked.**                                                                                                                                            |
| 8   | Personal defaults; prints and formats      | **Partly:** IMAX is in 14% of top threes; good free seats come up in the open answers.                                                                    |
| 9   | People follow kinds of screenings          | **Not asked.**                                                                                                                                            |
| –   | Would people use a chat?                   | **No:** nobody but the maintainer chose chat; 68% want a searchable list with filters.                                                                    |

## The survey and the focus group together

**They agree:**

- **Finnkino's redesign is the shared pain point**: 11 of 22 open-answer respondents, and both focus group participants.
- **One place for every cinema is wanted.** Usefulness averages 3.73 of 5, with 68% "very" or "extremely" useful. The frequent goers rate it higher (4.14 against 3.53, small groups).
- **Chat is not wanted for searching.** No survey respondent chose it; the focus group rejected it for search, though they wanted it for planning.
- **Programmes are known too late.** The survey says "next week isn't visible" and "only one week at a time"; the focus group said programmes are published on Tuesday to Thursday for the same week.
- **The seat matters.** Good free seats came up in the survey; "the best seat in the big hall" in the focus group.

**They differ:**

- **Film first or time first:** the survey, mostly occasional goers, is clearly film first (73%). The focus group, hobbyists, split one each. Occasional goers may go when a film they've heard of comes out; hobbyists also go because they have an evening free.
- **Following kinds of screenings and being told:** the strongest focus group finding, but the survey didn't ask about it, so there's no survey evidence either way.

**The survey adds:**

- **Location is as important as the start time.** This supports the radius search and the map.
- **"When does it leave the cinemas?"** is the same need as the "last showing" mark in journey B, which isn't built yet.
- **A list by start time, "like in the cinema lobby", with a radius slider**, which is close to the search page as built.

## Limits

- Convenience sample: mostly from one share on one day, from the Helsinki region, and mostly occasional moviegoers, so not our hobbyist audience.
- 22 answers: percentages move about 5 points per person.
- "Pick three" forces trade-offs: a factor left out (language) may still matter.
- The scales are Likert, not bipolar; the means describe this sample only.
- The questions about following, being notified, critics' scores and buying were not asked; those come from the focus group only.
